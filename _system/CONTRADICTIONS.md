# Contradictions Register

> Every `contradicts` edge in the vault must have a corresponding entry here.
> Updated by agents during `/convert-note` and `/vault-health` operations.

---

## Format

Each contradiction follows this structure:

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM-DD] Node A ↔ Node B — Status: open

- **Node A**: [[type/node-a]] — "summary of claim A"
- **Node B**: [[type/node-b]] — "summary of claim B"
- **Conflict**: What specifically contradicts between the two.
- **Detected by**: agent | human
- **Status**: open | investigating | resolved
- **Resolution**: _(document rationale when resolved)_
```
